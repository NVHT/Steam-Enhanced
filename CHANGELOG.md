## 1.5.6 · Nov 26, 2026

> Offline friends now really shows them in the dock, open or folded.

**Fixes**
- **Offline friends**: Shown lists offline friends in the dock (Steam's Offline section and your categories) and in the folded dock, behind a "+N"; Hidden keeps the dock as before. The Friends window lists everyone.
- **Friends list**: a folded Offline header no longer shows a "Sorting alphabetically" tip for a hidden button, and fills its row.
- **Rail**: a menu opened on hover no longer shows its button's tip over it.
- **Theme settings**: Logo place and the offline settings have their own icons.

<a name="v1-5-0"></a>

<details>
<summary><b>1.5.0</b> · Nov 25, 2026 · Every page of Steam's Settings has its own design: a card on top that shows what the page's settings do, drawn from Steam's own rows. Steam can start on a Store or Community page with the theme's Library beside it, the Friends window keeps its screen, and the whole theme went through a quality pass.</summary>
<br>

**Settings**
- **A card for every page**: Account, Friends & Chat, Family, Security, Notifications, Interface, Store, Library, Downloads, Storage, Cloud, In Game, Accessibility, Controller, Game Recording, Voice, Remote Play, Broadcast and Music each open with a card that shows the page's effect instead of repeating its rows: a preview, your games, a wireframe or a picture.
- **In Game**: the performance monitor and a toast drawn where they'll show, following the page's switches.
- **Accessibility**: a slice of the Library at your UI scale, with high contrast and reduced motion.
- **Controller**: Steam's own drawings of its hardware, lit for the kinds connected; a controller's Details shows its face buttons as games will (layout, Nintendo swap, universal glyphs, rumble); Advanced Settings maps which controller families go through Steam Input; Add a new controller shows the cable and the Puck's note.
- **Security**: Steam Guard's state as a phone with its code; Deauthorize marked as the dangerous one in any language.
- **Interface**: the start page and the beta badge read in any language.
- **Steam's Home**: with Home set to Steam's, the Home tab shows its own settings instead of the stage's: hide What's New or the Add shelf row.
- **One copy button**: Settings, Game Properties, group chat invites and System Information share one, ticked once copied.

**Client**
- **Start on a page**: with Start Up Location on the Store or Community, the Library loads once under the splash so the theme's game list is there beside the page, and the splash draws the page you start on.
- **Splash with Steam's Home**: Steam's Home drawn as the wireframe (What's New, a shelf, Add shelf) with the greeting and no game's art, in the startup window's background too (Splash art applies with the theme's Home). With no art, the words sit in the box's outline alone.
- **Toasts**: one card for every kind, with the Compact sample in Settings › Notifications in each kind's colour.
- **Menus over the Store and Community**: they open reliably, after Reload UI too.

**Fixes**
- **Friends window**: it reopens where you left it, on either screen, including a second screen with another scale (#4); after Reload UI it no longer reopens by itself.
- **Store**: the front page's event dots follow Steam's renamed class.
- **Announcements**: a post is dated by when it came out, not by its event's date, so a post about an event months ahead (Next Fest) no longer sits on top as new.
- **Game page**: clickable stats no longer show Steam's hover tile.
- **Wishlist**: a review summary the theme can't read is no longer shown as positive.
- **Focus rings**: back on the Store's front page tiles, navigation buttons, Next Fest and Replay.
- **Library Manager**: its reload asks first during a voice call, as the theme's Reload UI does.

**Under the hood**
- A release audit of every file changed since 1.4.0: dead code and CSS removed, raw colours, spacing, durations and blurs moved onto tokens, the web pages' controls onto the shared button, field, chip and card tokens, and stale comments rewritten.
- Fewer layout reads: the legal pages, purchase history, the Store's account and game pages and Community discussions no longer read layout on every scroll or change.
- Features clean up only their own marks when they unload: a JS reload (a theming aid) keeps the rail's buttons.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>1.4.0</b> · Nov 24, 2026 · The theme's settings move out of Millennium into a window of their own, with colour presets, new options for every area, and a pass over the whole theme for leaks, speed and consistency.</summary>
<br>

**Settings**
- **Theme settings**: a window of its own, from the rail's Settings menu or <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>`</kbd>, with a search and a tab per area. Changes apply at once, everywhere.
- **Moved from Millennium**: the options under Millennium → Themes → Steam Enhanced are gone; your choices there carry over on the first start.
- **Colours**: thirteen presets, or Custom to set each colour yourself, with a contrast mark on each.
- **New options**: high contrast, game art in dialogs, the rail's buttons, labels and hover menus, when the splash shows and its art, a still startup background, game page sections, rarity tiers, game icons, how the Friends dock starts and where chats open, friend cards and profile backgrounds, the toasts' place, time, queue and achievement art, the overlay's first view, panels, views, clock and play time, which sites are themed and page width.

**Fixes and polish**
- The overlay no longer opens blank when its last view was switched off.
- Achievements on a game page no longer leave observers behind.
- Friends' header art and avatars load for newer games, and Steam's "?" picture no longer shows as an avatar on Home.
- Rebuilt dialogs (About Steam, Add a Non-Steam Game, EULA, install, uninstall, System Information) show from their first frame.
- Search fields on Community guides and discussions are the theme's own, with a focus ring; the new-topic form shows focus too.
- Store account, wallet, phone, purchase history and licenses pages, sale trailers and Community buttons use the shared buttons, cards and menus.
- 32 px hit targets on the library's page arrows, Home's buttons, call tools and Store account pages.

**Under the hood**
- Theme updates no longer read layout while Steam changes the page: Home's stage, notifications, the friends list, the emoticon picker, Steam Settings, dialogs and SteamGridDB measure only when something resizes.
- Teardown cleans up everything it adds, for live reloads.
- Colours, corners, timing and stacking follow the theme's tokens throughout; dead rules and stale notes removed.

</details>

<summary><b>1.0.0</b> · Nov 13, 2026 · The Steam you always wanted. The first public release of Steam Enhanced, a Millennium theme that imagines what the Steam desktop client should look like.</summary>
<br>

**Client**
- **Library Home**: a cinematic hero stage over your recent games, with a glass tray of news, favourites and collections.
- **Game pages**: two columns, HowLongToBeat bars and achievement rarity tiers. Finish a game and it turns gold: *Mastered*.
- **Collections**: one view for one or all collections, grid or list, with drag and drop.
- **Friends dock**: grouped by game, collapsible to a slim rail, with Steam chat beside it.
